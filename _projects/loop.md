Created the layout for an artist presentation of one of their works. The work was inspired by the infinite scroll of social media apps with a physical display of a sculpture and a book that describes the reserach behind the work. My role was to create an editorial design that perfectly encapsulated the concept of the continuos image of the sculpture, while leaving space for the reader to understand the research and each aspect of it.
