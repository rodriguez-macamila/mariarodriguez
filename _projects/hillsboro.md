I provided comprehensive 3D visualization services for a large commercial retail park. Working from the client’s floor plans, I modelled the buildings and surrounding context with a high level of accuracy, producing renders used for marketing, tenant presentations, and planning. A key deliverable was a full walkthrough animation to communicate circulation and spatial experience to investors and potential tenants.
